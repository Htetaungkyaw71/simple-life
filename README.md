## Run locally

Node.js 22.12+ recommended. All runtime art is committed; Python is only needed to regenerate art.

```sh
npm ci
npm run dev
npm test
npm run build
npm run preview
```

Open the URL printed by Vite. Serve `dist/` over HTTP(S); relative paths support subdirectories. No backend, account, tracking, external API, remote runtime asset or CDN.

## Play

Choose boy/girl. Start with 60 coins, collect three Tomato seeds from the Home crate, and walk south through Home's map edge to Farm. Plant/water/harvest the starter beds, or select Shovel (4) and E/tap nearby clear Farm grass to dig another free bed. Adjacent beds join into connected soil; roads, water, bridges and structures cannot be dug. Water (2) pours on outdoor open ground and leaves a wet patch that dries after three active minutes or sleep; cosmetic wetness does not replace plant care. Farming costs are gentler: planting/digging/harvesting/watering 1 energy each. Water at **06:00–12:00 and 16:00–22:00**; one missed window wilts, two consecutive misses kill. One fully cared-for night yields two Tomatoes. Manual bed sleep at 06:00–20:00 wakes at 20:00; nighttime sleep wakes at 06:00, including the same morning after midnight. The clock rolls over continuously; crops/daily prices settle once at midnight. Elapsed time never drains energy. Zero energy still permits walking.

Twelve crops: Tomato / Strawberry / Carrot / Corn / Chili / Eggplant / Cucumber / Watermelon / Pumpkin / Grape / Blueberry / Sweet Potato. Original four values remain: seed prices **10 / 18 / 28 / 40**, growth **1 / 2 / 2 / 2** cared-for nights, yield **2 / 2 / 1 / 3**. The paged City seed shop supplies all twelve; values are in src/data/crops.js. Home's east edge leads to City. Clara sells seeds; Niko buys crops, forest fruit and Wild Hide; Rowan's southwest **OUTFITTER** sells one permanent auto-equipped **Wooden Club for 45 coins**. Merchants serve while at work during **07:00–20:00**. They leave early enough to reach their house doors by 20:00, and emerge at 06:00 to walk back. Shops pause simulation, use icon tabs, exact quotes and 1/5/Max or All quantities. City seed aid retains one free Tomato seed/day for eligible broke farmers.

Follow Home's wooden **FOREST ↑** sign to a **10240×5120 wilderness**, exactly **twice v0.4.8 and 40 times the original forest area** at the same camera/character scale. Forty connected regions include Homewood, jungle groves, savanna, bamboo, pine woods, marsh/river, basalt caves, ruins, highlands and a stylized snowy lake. Timber bridges cross the river; natural shores, walkable stone chambers, camp, shrine and ice landmarks reward exploring. The map icon / M opens the Forest-only parchment map, with explored terrain, rivers, player position and current facing. The ? control offers broad cache clues; exact treasure positions stay hidden. Head southwest to Homewood, then south to Home.

**323 daily forage nodes** provide nine fruits: Wild Berry (+12 energy / 6 base coins), Guava (+20 / 12), Mango (+30 / 20), Banana (+18 / 10), Orange (+22 / 14), Papaya (+32 / 22), Coconut (+35 / 24), Dragon Fruit (+42 / 36), Pineapple (+38 / 28). E/tap collects; Bag → food tab also offers farm-grown Watermelon (+35), Grape (+20) and Blueberry (+16), for thirteen foods including hunted Meat. Every food restores health as well as energy; the bag shows both gains. Meat restores 25 health / 20 energy and has a 16-coin base sale value. Eating consumes one, independently caps both stats at 100, and only refuses consumption when both are full (or health is zero). Market pages buy every fruit and Wild Hide with the retained 85–120% daily multiplier. Original crop/fruit/hide price salts stay unchanged. Nodes refresh once per day, never on scene reentry/reload.

**Only Craftpix animals remain:** 30 saved Wild Boars (two in Homewood, one per existing cache and seven new pairs), plus 128 quiet Deer/Hare/Fox/Grouse. Select Axe (5), then K or E / tap a nearby animal to hunt. Axe hits cost 1 energy with a 500ms cooldown; empty swings are free; boars and deer need three hits, foxes two, hares/grouse one. Boars/deer give two Meat; foxes/hares/grouse one. Boars also retain their Wild Hide drop. Defeated quiet animals stay removed across travel/reload and return on a later day. Boars need three club hits, deal 15 damage and charge at 280 units/second. They warn before charging, respect solids/territory and share player damage invulnerability. Club hits cost 2 energy with a 450ms cooldown. One Wild Hide and two Meat drop per defeated boar. Saved partial health/dead states survive travel; defeated boars return on a later day. Night awareness/movement increase slightly; distant areas remain dark.

**14 rare ancient caches** hide beyond the grassy passages in dangerous regions, including the Stone Hollow chamber. Approach to spot a chest; nearby living animals within 240 units block its recovery. Drive those guards away, then E / tap to recover 313–462 coins depending on the cache. Each cache pays once per save; sleep, reentry and reload cannot reroll it. MAP supplies broad clues after discovering the region, without revealing exact chest positions.

At zero health, fall/fade and wake beside the home bed at **06:00 next day**, with health/energy restored. Drop **floor(25% of carried coins)** into one saved Stash Bag at the safe forest defeat position. Return and E / tap it to recover the coins. A second defeat permanently replaces the old bag. Zero loss creates no empty bag. Crops, inventory, equipment and paid farms remain. First-visit and first-defeat messages explain the rules and pause safely.

- WASD/arrows: move; E: interact. Space: free outdoor jump; K: Forest directional attack. Legacy J/Shift jump keys remain available.
- 1/2/3/4/5/6/7: Seed / Water / Harvest / Shovel / Axe / Hand / Grass; Q: cycle planting crop. E / tap or a nearby ground click applies the selected tool.
- B / bag icon: seeds, produce, food and gear. Bag → Gear selects Shovel, Axe, Hand or Grass. The utility selector is hidden while Axe is selected. M / map icon: Forest-only map. Arrows/Tab/Enter navigate menus; Escape closes/pauses.
- Touch: circular analog joystick with Jump and Attack buttons, tools and direct screen taps. Select Axe in Bag → Gear, then Attack or tap the world to swing; Attack also chops a nearby tree. Jump is free outdoors; indoor action buttons are disabled. Menu/rotation/release/background reset movement. Double tap open Forest ground still jumps.
- Pause → Save, Forest sound ON/OFF, Controls, Resume or Return to title. Refresh → Continue.

Planting land is free: Shovel costs 1 energy per cell, with no coin purchase. Starter beds and previously purchased fields/farms remain. New beds can extend them across clear Farm grass; all planted beds receive the established care/growth rules. The Farm board offers a shovel guide, travel between previously owned farms and retained Tomato convenience trade.

Shovel and Axe are free tools available in Bag → Gear. Approach a tree, select Axe (5), then E/tap or click it: cutting costs 1 energy and gives 3 wood. Hand (6) gathers a nearby grass clump for 1 energy; Grass (7) places one collected clump on clear dry ground for 1 energy. Water, bridges, roads, solids and planted beds reject grass placement. Digging clears overlapping grass; gather it first to keep it. Tree roots/crowns disappear together, cuts stay removed across days/reloads and cut fruit trees stop producing fruit. Wood is stored in Gear. Forest now has 240 loose-stone piles, six per region. Select Hand (6) and E / tap a nearby pile: each costs 1 energy and gives 2 Stone. Collected piles stay removed across travel/reload and refresh on the next day. Stone is stored in Gear; no crafting is added.

Mana Seed, Modern Interiors Free and the retired non-CraftPix animal packs remain absent from production.

Primary ToffeeCraft/Channeechan/CraftPix pages were checked on 2026-10-10; retained LimeZu/Pipoya/Eris Esra evidence and prior download fingerprints reviewed. No creator contact, purchase, new external asset download, publication or submission was performed. The mixed atlas must not be redistributed as an asset library. This record resolves the known runtime ToffeeCraft uncertainty; final human team review, originality/rights declarations and organizer eligibility decisions remain pending. It is not a blanket legal certification.

The Mana Seed User License

As you may know, I sell Mana Seed assets in a variety of storefronts. Itchio, GameDev Market, The Unity Asset Store, Ko‑fi commissions, Patreon rewards, and other possibilities. No matter where you acquire my products, this user license applies. Below you'll find a brief summary with the most pertinent information, then you'll find the fine print and extended details. Please contact me if you have any questions (email at the bottom of the page).

Summary

You may...
  • ... use the asset(s) you bought to make one free or commercial video game.
  • ... share the asset with other people working on your video game.
  • ... edit my art in any way you see fit (it is actually encouraged).
  • ... credit me, though it is not a requirement.
You may not...
  • ... make print products (no posters, stickers, shirts, hats, mugs, etc).
  • ... resell or redistribute my art outside your team (they must acquire it from me, not you).
  • ... use my art in any sort of NFT, Web3, or GenAI project.
  • ... make more than one video game with the asset, unless you purchase it again.

Fine Print

  • All Rights Reserved - I (Seliel the Shaper) retain all copyright to any works I create. By purchasing one of my assets (or downloading any that are freely offered), I transfer to you limited commercial usage rights, as outlined below.
  • Single Product - You can use the assets you purchased (or acquired through patronage) in a SINGLE PRODUCT. That means one video game, or one YouTube video, or whatever your product is. If you'd like to use the asset you purchased again in a second product (that includes a sequel or DLC for the first product), you'll need to pay for it again. You may contact me directly for a discount on purchasing multiple product licenses.
  • DLC and Micro-transactions - Every instance of an individual purchase (or optional download, in the case of free content that isn't included for everyone automatically as part of the base game) would be considered a separate product (even if they're all released on the same day). So the initial game (even a free game) counts as one product. Then each item in your cash shop (so to speak) would be a different product. A single large content pack that a player buys once (or deliberately acquires for free, if it's optional) would be a single new product, but 10 outfit add-ons sold separately would be 10 different products. So, as an example, if you sold 5 of my hats from the Farmer Sprite System as micro-transactions in your cash shop (or on Steam, et al), you would need to repurchase the Farmer Sprite System 5 times to account for each of those "products". New free content that players get automatically (like a seasonal event in an MMO) would count as an update to the existing product, not a new product. The key here is that if the content is (or can be) acquired deliberately outside of normal gameplay (and no, a cash or premium currency shop is not gameplay), then it's a separate product.
  • Commercial Use - The "product" in question MAY be a commercial product you charge money for.
  • Printed Products - The "product" in question may NOT be a printed product (nor a digital product made for the consideration of printing). No posters, stickers, shirts, hats, mugs, etc. If you want to use my art for a board or card game or your video game's printed promotional materials, please reach out to me to seek written authorization. This prohibition is here to prevent low-effort middlemen from trying to profit by flipping my art. If you're trying to think of some printed product that I might authorize, consider that if it's something you can throw together in a few hours and put up on a print-on-demand site, then it would definitely be in violation of this user license.
  • Game Trailers - If you're making a video game, THAT is your "product". Hence, you may publish multiple trailers, developer streams, demos, etc, for the video game, and they would still fall under the umbrella of the single associated video game "product".
  • Tutorials - If your "product" is a series of YouTube tutorials, showing people how to make games in Unity (for example), I would consider the entire series to be the single product, so you would not need to repurchase the asset for every video it appears in. Though please note the section on Reselling & Redistribution: you cannot give those tutorial assets to your viewers, they must be acquired directly from me via my storefronts, even if they're free to download.
  • Free Assets - The one-product limitation does not apply to assets listed as "free" on itch.io, or the "Seliel's Sketchpad" assets exclusive to Patreon or Ko-fi. You can use those as many times as you like.
  • Reselling & Redistribution - You may NOT resell or redistribute (either commercially or freely) my assets for other people to make their own products. They need to acquire it from me, not you. Even if you recolor, resize, or change the assets in some way as mentioned previously, this is still not permitted.
  • Editing Assets - You MAY edit, recolor, or change the assets in any way you see fit. Please feel free to erase, move, rotate, resize, or do whatever you need to best suit your product.
  • Multiple Users - If you have multiple team members (coders, artists, etc.) working on a single project (your video game), you don't need to buy a license for each of them. Just once for the project itself.
  • Transferability - This license is non-transferable without explicit written permission from Seliel the Shaper (email below). If you have a special circumstance (like a closing studio passing an in-progress game to another studio), please contact me to discuss.
  • Outside Commissions - If you hire a freelance artist to make edits to my artwork, you may share with them any assets you have purchased from me, a separate purchase is not required. That artist may not use assets acquired this way for any other purpose, ONLY your product.
  • Freelancers - If YOU are a freelance artist, and you want to use Mana Seed assets to create something for a client, you must communicate that to the client and get them to purchase the asset for you to use. The reason for this is due to the single-license clause outlined above: since my assets can only be used for a single "product" per purchase, that asset should be purchased by and belong (in full) to the "product" owner (to use an example: you can't purchase an asset once and use part of it for one client's project and part of it for another).
  • No GenAI - I do not consent for any of my art to be used in any machine learning datasets, nor used in a project alongside "AI" generated imagery, writing, code, or anything else. I am morally opposed to this technology for many reasons, please do not ask me if your use-case is special; it is not. Don't settle for computer-generated slop. Support real artists, and make a better game in the process!
  • No Web3 - I am vehemently opposed to anything related to NFTs, cryptocurrency, or blockchain technology in general. You are expressly prohibited from using my art in any web3-related product. No exceptions.
  • No Bigotry - I reserve the right to disassociate myself and the Mana Seed brand from any products or persons I find morally repugnant. If you're making something racist, sexist, homo/transphobic, a deliberate scam, or environmentally destructive, then don't expect me to support your product or let you into my Discord. I may even speak out publicly against it, which may hurt your sales.
  • Mana Seed Compatible Assets - If you're an artist looking to make and SELL assets that are compatible with mine (like outfits for my Character Base or Farmer Sprite System), I might be OK with it under certain circumstances, but PLEASE get in touch with me first. There are some things I might not be OK with, so please talk to me first and get my approval before labeling something "Mana Seed" compatible. If you find someone selling an asset online advertised as Mana Seed compatible, and it's not on this list, then that person has not cleared their product with me. Please do not give them any money. There are some baseline rules you should understand before you start such a project:
    • You can only sell the assets on itch.io.
    • You need to have an itch.io embed for the compatible product near the top of the page.
    • You can't use the Mana Seed logo or similar page color palette.
    • You'll need to provide me with a download key so I can check on the quality and compatibility.
    • I need to look over your page and provide feedback BEFORE you go public.
  • Credit - No, you don't have to credit me, though I would appreciate it 💖 Just don't claim you drew it yourself. If you tell me about your product I can help spread the word on social media.
  • Something Else? - If there's anything you think I haven't covered in this list, please contact me (email below) so I can clarify or update the license. Thank you for your understanding!
If you experience any confusion or frustration on account of any part of this license, please reach out to me with your concerns (I'm Seliel the Shaper at gee mail dot com). I'm sure we can find a pleasant solution for everyone. Happy world-building!

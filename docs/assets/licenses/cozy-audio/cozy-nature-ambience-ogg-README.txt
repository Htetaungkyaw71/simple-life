Cozy Nature Ambience — 12 seamless ambience loops for cozy games

Background ambience for cozy games: weather, nature and places. Each loop is 30 seconds
and repeats with no gap or click (set your audio player to loop). Layer two or three
(rain + fireplace, forest + river) to build your own scene.

Formats: wav/ (44.1 kHz, 16-bit, stereo) and ogg/ (Ogg Vorbis, loops perfectly in Godot,
Unity, GameMaker and the web).

  Weather
    rain-light  (gentle rain, for cozy rooms and rainy days)
    rain-storm  (heavy rain with distant thunder)
    meadow-wind  (soft wind over a meadow, a few birds)
    winter-wind  (cold howling wind for snowy maps)

  Nature
    forest-day  (birdsong and rustling leaves)
    night-crickets  (crickets, a far owl, a calm night)
    river-stream  (a bubbling stream over stones)
    ocean-waves  (slow waves on a beach)

  Places
    cave-drips  (water drops echoing in a cave)
    campfire  (crackling campfire outdoors)
    fireplace-room  (fireplace and a ticking clock indoors)
    town-fountain  (a fountain in the town square, distant bell)

How they were made: synthesized by our own code from filtered noise, resonators and small
events placed on a circular buffer, so the end flows into the start. No recorded samples.

Music that fits: Cozy Lofi Beats (aquumgifts.itch.io/cozy-lofi-beats) and Cozy Farm Music
(aquumgifts.itch.io/cozy-farm-music). See LICENSE.txt for what you can do with these.

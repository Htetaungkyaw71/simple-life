The user’s screenshots show a disabled, faint Place button and the “Move the preview closer” restriction. Outdoor-only pieces still cannot enter the room, Forest or City. The global 96-piece cap remains for additions.

Click or touch a placed piece, with any tool, to open the canvas **Remove / Readjust / Cancel** dialog. Nearby Hand/Use also opens it. Selection itself spends/removes nothing. Remove recovers one piece into Bag/Gear and fails safely when its item stack is full. Readjust keeps the original record saved/colliding while hiding its drawing and previewing its proposed position/direction. Add atomically replaces the original record without extra coins, inventory stock or a spare decoration slot. Cancel, tool/crop change, opening Bag/another modal or reload preserves the original; room rotation ends readjust before the established proportional relocation/repacking. Ordinary mobile world touches still cannot farm, gather, travel or attack; only decoration selection gets this explicit editing path.
